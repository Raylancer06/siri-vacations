The Scotland of India Beckons

Tucked into the misty folds of Karnataka's Western Ghats, Kodagu (Coorg) is a timeless sanctuary of rolling hills, aroma-rich coffee plantations, cascading cascades, and warm Kodava hospitality. With Siri Vacations' bespoke chauffeur-driven holiday, escape Bangalore’s bustle for three curated days of sensory rejuvenation.

From traversing the tranquil forest trails of Cauvery Nisargadhama and exploring the spiritual serenity of the Golden Tibetan Temple in Bylakuppe, to soaking in the roaring Abbey Falls and capturing golden hour from Raja’s Seat—every moment is designed for effortless leisure and unforgettable family memories.

Day-Wise Detailed Itinerary

1
Day 01 • Approx 260 KM Breakfast Included

Bengaluru to Coorg via Bylakuppe & Nisargadhama

expand_more

Early morning private pickup from your doorstep or Bengaluru Airport in a sanitised sedan/SUV. Cruise down the Mysuru expressway towards Kushalnagar, enjoying the transition from urban landscapes to the aromatic hills of Kodagu.

wb_twilight Morning (06:00 - 11:30)

Pickup from Bengaluru, scenic highway breakfast stop, drive past Srirangapatna.

sunny Afternoon (12:00 - 15:30)

Visit Bylakuppe Namdroling Monastery, Tibetan flea market and Cauvery Nisargadhama bamboo island.

bedtime Evening (16:30 onwards)

Check-in at luxury estate resort/homestay. Fresh plantation coffee welcome drink. Leisure evening by campfire.

Sightseeing: Golden Temple Nisargadhama Deer Park Plantation Resort
2
Day 02 • Sightseeing Tour Breakfast Included

Abbey Falls, Madikeri Fort & Sunset at Raja's Seat

expand_more

Wake up to the birdsong of Malabar whistling thrushes. Savor freshly brewed estate coffee before setting out for Madikeri’s celebrated architectural landmarks, lush waterfalls, and unforgettable valley sunsets.

wb_twilight Morning (09:00 - 12:30)

Visit Abbey Falls nestled amid coffee estates; walk through spice plantations with private guide.

sunny Afternoon (13:30 - 16:30)

Tour Madikeri Fort museum and the unique Gothic-Islamic Omkareshwara Temple built in 1820.

bedtime Evening (17:00 - 19:30)

Catch magical sunset panoramic views over the cliffs of Raja's Seat followed by musical fountain show.

Sightseeing: Abbey Falls Omkareshwara Temple Madikeri Fort Raja's Seat
3
Day 03 • Approx 250 KM Breakfast Included

Dubare Elephant Camp & Return Drive to Bengaluru

expand_more

Check-out after a traditional South Indian breakfast. Visit Dubare for elephant observation along the Cauvery bank, indulge in homemade chocolate and pure honey shopping, and return safely to Bengaluru.

wb_twilight Morning (08:30 - 11:30)

Dubare Elephant Camp: Boat ride across river Cauvery to witness elephant bathing and feeding sessions.

sunny Afternoon (12:00 - 14:00)

Spice and coffee powder shopping in Madikeri town; authentic Coorg pork curry / veg lunch stop.

bedtime Evening (14:30 - 20:30)

Relaxing chauffeur drive back to Bengaluru. Drop-off at your residence or Kempegowda International Airport.

Sightseeing: Dubare Camp Local Spice Market Bengaluru Drop

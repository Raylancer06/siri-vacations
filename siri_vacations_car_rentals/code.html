Frequently Asked Questions on Car Rentals

Everything you need to know about our chauffeur-driven rental service.

How is the outstation fare calculated?

expand_more
Outstation fares are charged on a round-trip basis with a standard minimum billing of 300 kilometers per calendar day. The total charge comprises: (Total KM x Rate/KM) + (Number of days x Daily Driver Allowance) + actual toll taxes, state permits, and GST.

What is Driver Batta / Driver Allowance?

expand_more
Driver batta covers the chauffeur's daily food and accommodation expenses (typically ₹400 for sedans, ₹500 for Innova Crysta, and ₹600-700 for luxury vehicles per calendar day). You do not need to provide room or meals for the chauffeur at resorts.

Do you handle inter-state road taxes for Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Andhra Pradesh?

expand_more
Yes! When crossing Karnataka borders into Wayanad (Kerala), Ooty (Tamil Nadu), or Tirupati (Andhra Pradesh), our drivers carry all legitimate commercial permits and pay the necessary state commercial entry taxes online before arrival to prevent roadside checkpoint delays.

Can I hire a car for local Bengaluru meetings or airport pickups?

expand_more
Yes, we offer local Bengaluru 4Hr/40Km, 8Hr/80Km, and 12Hr/120Km packages, as well as fixed airport drop and pickup transfers to Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) in sedans, Innova Crystas, and Hycross MPVs.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

expand_more
Free cancellations are permitted up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled departure time with 100% refund. For cancellations within 12 hours of pickup, a nominal token dispatch charge applies.
